# 5-quote outreach sprint

Goal: **5 treasury-operator conversations** (15 min each) before Oct 9, logged
with verbatim pain. This is the highest-leverage remaining work: it converts
the Validation spine item from "survey citations" to "five humans," which is
exactly what "Metrics / Validation" judges.

Rules: no fake quotes, ever. Every touch below is real, sendable, and honest
about being a builder doing research. Log everything — including non-responses.

## Channel plan (highest yield first)

### 1. HackCanton mentors + Discord (warmest, do first)

Mentors see project progress and give continuous feedback — asking them for a
treasury intro is literally what they are there for.

**Discord DM / mentor channel post:**

> Building NetSettle for Track 1 (intercompany netting: demo compresses
> $312k gross → $40k net across 4 subsidiaries, settles atomically on Canton,
> exports a bank payment file). Live demo: [URL]. Question for mentors: does
> anyone have a path to a corporate treasury operator for a 15-min research
> call? Not selling — need to hear how the monthly cycle actually breaks. Will
> share notes back with the cohort.

### 2. Canton Forum (public, judge-visible effort)

Post in Outreach/discussion: 3-min demo + the 5 questions from VALIDATION.md +
"office hours" offer (two 30-min slots this week, first come). A public thread
is itself evidence of validation effort, win or lose.

**Forum post skeleton:**

> Title: [Research] How painful is your intercompany netting cycle? (15-min calls)
>
> I'm building NetSettle (Track 1): multilateral intercompany netting on
> Canton — [one-paragraph product + live demo URL + gross→net numbers].
> Surveys say 88% of finance teams struggle with payment ops and netting can
> cut cross-border transfers ~70%, but I have zero operator conversations and
> I don't trust my own assumptions. If you run or touch a monthly netting
> cycle (treasury, SSC, intercompany accounting), I'll take 15 minutes whenever
> suits you: [2 time slots]. Five questions, no pitch, notes shared back here.

### 3. LinkedIn DMs (volume play: 20 touches → ~5 conversations)

Targets, in order: (a) ex-treasury now consulting/advising (highest response),
(b) SSC / intercompany accountants at multinationals, (c) TMS implementers
(GTreasury/Kyriba/ION consultants — they live inside netting projects),
(d) group treasurers at mid-caps (lowest response, try 3–4 anyway).

**DM template (under 100 words):**

> Hi [name] — [one personal line: their post / role / project]. I'm building a
> tool for the monthly intercompany netting cycle (compress subsidiary payables
> to net settlements, atomic commit, bank-ready payment file out). Live demo:
> [URL]. Could I take 15 min to hear how the cycle breaks from the inside?
> Not selling anything — I'm in a Canton hackathon and trying to earn the
> problem before I claim the solution.

Follow-up once, after 4 days, one line. Then move on.

### 4. X / Canton builder community

Reply to @appsfactory_cc threads + Canton builder posts with the demo and one
specific question ("for those who've run netting cycles: disputes or late
invoices — which stalls you more?"). Low yield, near-zero cost.

### Skip (low yield or gated)

- ECTN (corporate-treasurers-only, you likely don't qualify), ACT forums
  (member-gated), cold email to generic treasury inboxes, paid panels.
